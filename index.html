Est. 1920s — The Oscar Kincaid Property

A century-old stand, back in business.

Oscar Kincaid Sr. built this little roadside ice cream stand in the 1920s. Then it went quiet — and stayed quiet for 26 years.

In the summer of 2026, the Dowling family brought it back to life: same good bones, fresh white paint, a hand-painted sign, and the smell of sourdough drifting toward the road again.

1920s
Oscar Kincaid Sr. builds a little ice cream stand on the family property.
Mid-century
A familiar sight on Powerville Road.
26 quiet years
The stand sits idle, waiting for its next chapter.
Summer 2026
Restored & reopened by the Dowling family, ribbon cut with Boonton Township.
